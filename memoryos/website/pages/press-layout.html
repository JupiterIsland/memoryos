DECEMBER 2006 • UNCUT MAGAZINE

Paul Draper On The Break-Up Of Mansun & Solo Recovery

The final sessions of Kleptomania, Paul's illness and recovery, and building his own studio path.

"One night it all kicked off. We broke into the wine cellar, picked out the best bottle of champagne and got twatted. There was a lot of shouting and aggro. Chad punched his hand into a brick wall and ended up in hospital on a morphine drip. People were saying that I was a paranoid schizophrenic. In fact with all the stress I got a malignant tumour and had to have a course of radiation therapy before having it cut out." — Paul Draper
