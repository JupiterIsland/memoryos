Why Staying Connected Matters

RJMedia exists because I stayed online.

Every archive update.

Every video upload.

Every piece of documentation.

Every community response.

All of it depends on reliable technology.

Like many people across the UK, I experienced housing instability and found myself living in temporary accommodation while continuing to maintain archive projects and community resources.

AI helped me stay organised, document information and continue building.

Today I am still here, still building and still preserving material because technology enabled me to remain connected.
