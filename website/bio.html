Canonical Biography

Richard John Murray is a technical systems builder based in Carlisle, England. He designs and runs a unified AI infrastructure built from three core systems: MemoryOS, Jupiter One Hardware Reality, and The Quiet Advisor.

MemoryOS handles GPU-aware memory routing, tier management, and checkpoint persistence. Jupiter One provides real-time VRAM analysis, hardware ROI recommendations, and multi-model scheduling. The Quiet Advisor observes inference patterns, runs ten detector algorithms nightly, and generates one actionable insight per day based on real usage data.

His work focuses on inference optimization, GPU efficiency, multi-model orchestration, and exposing hidden patterns in user workflows — building systems that reveal waste, improve throughput, and eliminate guesswork across Docker, WSL2, Linux, Windows automation, consumer GPUs, and competitive model testing. The ecosystem consists of a memory engine, a hardware reality layer, a GPU inference runtime, and a daily insight system that teaches users how to use their own tools better.

Alongside his systems engineering work, Richard has preserved live music history and fan communities since founding the MansunMusicLovers group in December 2001 and running Paul-Draper.net / pauldraper-music.co.uk. In August 2014, he filmed the first public playback of Paul Draper's debut solo song ("Feeling My Heart Run Slow") at the Mansun convention in Chester — a recording credited by Louder Than War to "Richard Murray of the Paul-Draper.net fansite", named by Vanyaland, and featured by The Guardian in its indie playlist.
