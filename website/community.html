WHY KEEP THE CONNECTION EQUALS THE ARCHIVE

Mission 1 preserves recordings. Mission 2 preserves people.

Behind every performance, documentary, photograph and recording is a personal story. Like many people across the UK, Richard John Murray experienced housing instability while continuing to maintain 25 years of music archives and community resources.

Using AI tools from temporary accommodation to organise evidence, document Section 188 housing duties, and advocate for safe, pet-friendly housing proved that reliable connectivity is not a luxury — it is a lifeline.

If you are experiencing housing difficulties, digital exclusion, connectivity problems, or need help organising information with AI, reach out directly: richardmurray2022@gmail.com
