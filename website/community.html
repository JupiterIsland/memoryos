CARLISLE, ENGLAND • TECHNICAL SYSTEMS BUILDER & ARCHIVIST

RICHARD JOHN MURRAY

Technical Systems Builder in Carlisle, England. I design and run a unified AI infrastructure built from three core systems: MemoryOS, Jupiter One Hardware Reality, and The Quiet Advisor.

MemoryOS handles GPU-aware memory routing, tier management, and checkpoint persistence. Jupiter One provides real-time VRAM analysis, hardware ROI recommendations, and multi-model scheduling. The Quiet Advisor observes inference patterns, runs ten detector algorithms nightly, and generates one actionable insight per day based on real usage data.

My work focuses on inference optimization, GPU efficiency, multi-model orchestration, and exposing hidden patterns in user workflows across Docker, WSL2, Linux, Windows automation, consumer GPUs, and competitive model testing — building systems that reveal waste, improve throughput, and eliminate guesswork.

I also filmed the first public playback of Paul Draper's debut solo song at the Mansun convention in Chester. Louder Than War credited the recording to "Richard Murray of the Paul-Draper.net fansite", Vanyaland named me as the person who captured it, and The Guardian featured the video in its indie playlist.
