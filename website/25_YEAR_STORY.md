# Music Archive — 25 Years of Community Trust

## The Story

In 1999, I started a Yahoo Group to discuss Paul Draper and Mansun with fans.

**25 years later, that community is still with me.**

We've moved platforms a dozen times:
- Yahoo Group (1999-2005)
- Invision Board (2007-2024)
- YouTube (2023-2024)
- Now: AI-powered archive platform

**People didn't follow the tech. They followed the mission.**

## The Mission

Preserve 25 years of music history that would otherwise disappear.

Not just Paul Draper. Not just Mansun.
But the **culture** around music from 1999-2024.

- Live performances (rare footage)
- Artist interviews (insight)
- Fan discussions (community memory)
- News coverage (historical record)
- Photos (visual archive)
- Liner notes + context (why it matters)

## Why AI Changes Everything

For 25 years, we organized manually:
- Hand-tag forum posts
- Date-sort photos
- Manually write descriptions
- Remember where things are

**AI lets us:**
- Gemini watches every video → understands context
- Auto-generate liner notes → preserve why it matters
- Searchable across 25 years → find anything instantly
- Connected timeline → see how music evolved
- Preserved forever → decentralized, open-source
