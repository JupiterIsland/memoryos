2006 • KOMAKINO.HTML • EXCLUSIVE STUDIO DIARY BY RYAN

.: KOMAKINO AND PAUL (PARR STREET & DRAPOSOUND DIARY) :.

By Ryan (Komakino vocalist) — Written exclusively for PaulDraper-Music.co.uk:

"At the moment we have recorded two songs with Paul as producer — 'Every Day Is Like a Friday Night' and 'Kommunikate'. We met up with Paul in Chester after Steve Lamacq told him that we were looking for a producer and suggested he get in touch with us. It was fucking weird when we got the email from Paul as we'd been joking about asking him to record us, because being Mansun fans we knew that he'd produced '...Grey Lantern' and 'Six' and we all really love the sound of those records. The guitarists in our band (twins Stuart and Martin) were even in the Mansun fan club back when they first started out!"
"Before we started Paul came up to Derby for a couple of days and we bashed through the songs loads of times to try out different structures, rhythmic changes and tempos. We then tried out loads of different guitar and amp combinations and drum/percussion ideas to really nail the sound that Paul could hear and wanted to get into the tracks — he's very much a perfectionist when it comes to that stuff and is really fucking knowledgeable about it all. After that we went up to Liverpool Parr Street Studio for 4 days and did all the instrumental recording, doing the journey from Chester — through the Mersey tunnel — to Liverpool and back every day."
"The following week I went to Paul's house near London for a few days and did all the vocals in his home studio set-up... I learnt fucking loads and it was really cool to work on vocal stuff with Paul."
2005 — 2006 • COLLABORATIONS.HTML • FAKE CHEMICAL STATE

.: PAUL DRAPER & SKIN ('ALONE IN MY ROOM') :.

Following Mansun's split, Paul Draper travelled to France in early 2004 to write and produce with ex-Skunk Anansie frontwoman Skin for her solo album Fake Chemical State. The lead download single 'Alone In My Room' (Paul's first post-Mansun release) was written and produced by Paul Draper and Skin, featuring Paul on guitars and bass and Wayne Riches on drums.

DRAPERBIOGRAPHY.HTML • PROFILE BY DOMINIQUE (HANIBUL)

.: PAUL DRAPER BIOGRAPHY :.

Back in November 1996 I re-read an interview with Mansun that made me fall hopelessly in love with Paul Draper. Ironically enough it wasn't Paul's words that caused this, it was the journalist's: "They've already lost three members, catch Mansun now before they become a solo artist." And I thought, "That'll be Paul." It struck me as admirable that he was perceived as someone who never gives up on what he believes in, on what he loves doing.

He's a quiet lad but very caring and thoughtful. During the Mansun years he kept fans happy with personalised letters, after-show meet-ups and free singles for Christmas. In 1997, he was asked to co-front a BBC Radio 1 campaign encouraging people with mental health issues to seek advice via The Samaritans — chosen because he was well known and had a warm and friendly voice.

Paul was born in Liverpool to his parents, Mr and Mrs Draper, who brought him up in Connah's Quay, North Wales alongside his two older sisters. Ever the slave to his peripatetic lifestyle, Paul has also resided in Chester, Nottingham and Weybridge. He has never forgotten his roots and remains a staunch Evertonian who knows a lot about midfield transfers. You could always tell when Paul's mum and dad were in the audience as he wouldn't shout "FUCKIN'!!" in Taxloss.

— Thanks to Dominique for writing this profile for PaulDraper-Music.co.uk.
