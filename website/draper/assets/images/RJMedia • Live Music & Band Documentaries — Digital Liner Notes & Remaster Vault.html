🎛️ RJMEDIA TOPAZ VIDEO AI MASTER REFERENCE

Expert Restoration Pathways & CLI Mapping

Quick-reference matrix for hobbyists remastering low-bitrate digital footage, fast 1080p-to-4K concerts, and 1990s interlaced VHS tapes.

Pathway 01: Low-Quality Digital

For blocky 360p/480p MPEG-2 or early web rips.

  • • Model: Iris MQ (or Nyx 1x ➔ Proteus 2x)
  • • Scale: Max 2x per pass
  • • Revert Comp: 55 to 70
  • • Improve Detail: 15 to 25
  • • Reduce Noise: 15 to 25
  • • Dehalo: 15 to 30
  • • Recover Detail: 35 to 50
Pathway 02: Fast 1080p to 4K

Bypasses internal 4x-to-8K penalty for max FPS.

  • • Model: Proteus v4 or Artemis HQ
  • • Multiplier: Native 2x (Focus Fix OFF)
  • • Revert Comp: 25 | Detail: 35
  • • Sharpen: 30 | Noise: 10
  • • Anti-Alias/Deblur: -15
  • • Recover Detail: 50
  • • Grain: OFF (Use Prenoise: 2)
Pathway 03: 1990s Analog VHS

Crop 12px bottom static + Bob Deinterlace to 59.94p/50p.

  • • Pass 1 (1x Clean): Iris LQ (Comp 70, Noise 40, Halo 50, Deblur +20, Sharp 0)
  • • Pass 2 (2x Upscale): Proteus v4 to 1440x1080 (Comp 15, Detail 40, Sharp 35, AA -10)
  • • Post Grain: Amount 3-4, Size 2
